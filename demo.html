TouchControls

Controls for touch displays & smartboards

Try the toolbar, bottom-left.

Pen

Tap to draw · tap again = next colour · once more = off

Long-press the pen = erase this slide

The button shows the active colour; marks clear on the next slide.

Zoom

Tap the magnifier, then tap the spot to enlarge.

Tap again to zoom back out.

More

Pause blanks the screen · Overview shows all slides · Fullscreen

The toolbar fades out when idle and returns on movement.

On dark slides the buttons turn white.
