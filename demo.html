TouchControls

Controls for touch displays & smartboards

Try the toolbar, bottom-left.

Pen

Tap to draw · tap again = next colour · once more = off

Long-press the pen = erase this slide

Marks stay put — leave this slide, come back, they are still here

The button shows the active colour. Marks live until you erase them or reload the page. Only the first touch draws — palms and extra fingers are ignored.

Whiteboard

Tap the board for a white writing surface — the pen switches on by itself.

Tap again (or change slides) to close it.

Great for spontaneous sketches and collecting ideas.

Focus

Tap the magnifier, then a cell below — the surroundings dim, the context stays.
Tap again zooms in on it · drag moves the spotlight · a third tap resets.

Timer

Tap the timer to cycle 5 · 10 · 15 minutes — one more tap turns it off.

The countdown stays visible for the whole room and pulses red at 0:00.

It keeps running across slide changes — perfect for group work.

More

Pause blanks the screen · Overview shows all slides · Fullscreen

The toolbar fades out when idle and returns on movement.

On dark slides the buttons turn white.
